Binds: all sim-core systems. Prevents: bevy_ecs parallel-executor nondeterminism breaking NFR3/scenario harness. Parallel scheduling = future swap gated on a profiled problem.
- (decision) Delta production: single tile-mutation path (World::set_tile) records per-tick dirty set exposed by sim-core; simd delta = dirty tiles + ALL entity states every tick (no entity diffing at phase-one scale). Prevents: two delta mechanisms / missed-mutation desync. Chattiness sanctioned by ADR3.
