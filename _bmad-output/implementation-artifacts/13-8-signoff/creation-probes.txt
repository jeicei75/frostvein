C. clear_paths (lib.rs:1622) removes EVERY entity's Path, idle exit paths included. It is called
   from every dig and cut completion (:1348, :2552) and from :3167. An arrival that walks in on an
   idle Path loses that Path at the first dig anywhere. With home = camp it then has no wander
   candidate (every neighbour is outside WANDER_RADIUS of home) and freezes where it stands. The
   release_claim NOTE (:1596-1606) describes the same freeze.
