read sustained fps from the frame-time overlay; do not substitute a devpod frame rate.

```text
gui.exe --subdiv 4
gui.exe --subdiv 4 --distance 500
gui.exe --subdiv 8
gui.exe --subdiv 8 --distance 500
```
